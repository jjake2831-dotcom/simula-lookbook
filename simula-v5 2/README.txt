SIMULA / PINAGMULAN — EDITORIAL WEBSITE, REFINED EDITION

Open index.html in a modern browser. No build tools needed. Google Fonts require an internet connection; fallback fonts are included.

Features: six distinct AI-generated garment visuals, interactive gallery and garment archive, garment hotspots, a personal lookbook selection, chapter navigation, textile-to-garment journey, sales model, market positioning, sources and a closing brand pitch.

This is an academic brand concept and editorial catalog, not a live commerce website. AI-generated images are illustrative and do not document real community textiles or existing partnerships.

To publish, upload the contents of the simula-v5 folder to a static host, with index.html at the root.
